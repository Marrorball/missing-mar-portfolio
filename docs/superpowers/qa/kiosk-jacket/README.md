# Recognisable hanging jacket

The grey ellipsoid next to the right-wall calendar was the seller's jacket,
not a pipe. Removed that stand-in and its block hook from the interior batch.

`props.hanging_jacket` now models a compact waist-length jacket with shaped
shoulders, two separate hanging sleeves, ribbed cuffs/hem, an open stand
collar, zipper teeth and pull, slanted pocket openings and sewn outlines.
It hangs on a wooden hanger from a screwed wall hook. Smooth elliptical
sections and small folds replace the original faceted blob.

The jacket hugs the right wall and sits a little closer to the back corner,
at Y=0.58 m, clear of the calendar and the door route. No camera, navigation,
other furnishing or calendar changes.

with the existing zoom control widened. The sleeves, zipper and pockets are
visible beside the calendar.
