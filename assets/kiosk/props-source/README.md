The Snickers logo is taken without redrawing from the official product image:
https://www.snickers.com/sites/g/files/fnmzdf616/files/migrate-product-files/dryeqrv2efldaaoyceat.png
https://www.snickers.com/

Other packaging artwork and the plaid are original procedural textures. These are evocative decorative props, not replicas of dated retail packs.
Rebuild textures with Pillow: `python scripts/build-props-textures.py`.

Calendar photo: t.A.T.u. (Yulia Volkova and Lena Katina), 2003, by NatureBoyMD.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:TatuGroupPhoto.jpg
The author released it into the public domain, so no credit is needed on the site; kept here for the record.
`scripts/build-props-textures.py` crops it from the heads to the belts and prints it onto the one calendar
sheet (calendar-june-2004.jpg) with the June 2004 grid, May and July in small, the binding holes and the hanger notch.
The month is the user-requested June 2004 with the 30th circled (birthday).
